**Glowline 0.9.40 Alpha**

### New

- **Floating mini lyrics.** A small always-on-top window with the current and next line, for gaming or working without full screen. Drag it anywhere; it hides itself while the full-screen view is in front. Turn it on in Settings → Extras or with `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M`.
- **Global shortcuts** that work from any app: `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L` opens Glowline, `M` toggles the mini lyrics, `←`/`→` shift the timing by 0.25 s and `S` shares the current line. Settings shows which ones are taken by other apps.
- **Share a line as an image.** Right-click any line in full screen → *Share as image*. You get a 1080×1350 card with the cover, its colors and the line, copied to the clipboard and saved in `Pictures\Glowline`.
- **Per-line timing.** Right-click a line to make just that line start earlier or later. Glowline remembers it for that song.
- **OBS overlay.** Settings → Extras → *OBS overlay* gives you a link to add as a Browser source: transparent background, current line, next line and cover, with URL options for size, alignment and style. It only accepts connections from your own computer.
- **Discord status.** Turn on Settings → Extras → *Show what I'm listening to on Discord* and your profile shows "Listening to Glowline" with the song, artist and a progress bar. It only talks to the Discord app on your computer; nothing to set up.
- **Romanization** for Korean, Japanese (kana) and Cyrillic lyrics, shown under each line. It's worked out on your device, with no internet needed.
- **Manual search across all three sources.** Search results now include Lrcmux and LyricsPlus besides LRCLIB (type `Artist - Title` to look for a different song). Picks from Lrcmux/LyricsPlus are kept for that song and don't expire with the cache.

### Improved

- Smoother full screen: the song key is no longer recomputed with a regex twice per frame.
- The lyrics cache is now written safely and cleaned up automatically (it used to grow forever).
- The updater removes old installers (~150 MB each) and interrupted downloads.
- The log keeps the previous file as `log.old.txt` instead of deleting it.
- Escaped characters (`é`, emoji) in `.lyricsfile` lyrics now display correctly.
- Requests now identify the app as `Glowline/<version>` instead of an outdated name.

### Under the hood

- Source code for 0.9.30 and 0.9.40 is now on GitHub.
- 42 automated tests and a Windows build on every push (GitHub Actions).
- `FullscreenWindow` split into smaller files.

### Downloads

- **Windows installer:** `LyricGlowSetup-0.9.40.exe` — **recommended.**
- **Windows portable:** `Glowline-0.9.40-portable.exe` — standalone, no installation.
- **Android:** no new APK in this release; the experimental APK from 0.9.30 still works. The new extras (mini lyrics, shortcuts, OBS, sharing, Discord) are Windows-only for now.
